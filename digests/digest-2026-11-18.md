## Interactive Learning Environments (2편)

### 1. Exploring characteristics of primary school students’ behaviors and interaction qualities in human-GenAI collaborative creative problem-solving activities
- 저자: Jie Xu, Jiayue Shi, Wenli Chen, Dan Sun, Yan Li  |  게재: 2026-11-17
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2762378](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2762378)

**초록:**
Creative problem-solving (CPS) is essential for addressing complex and uncertain problems, yet it is challenging for learners to develop high-quality solutions. Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) offers new possibilities for supporting CPS by facilitating divergent and convergent thinking. However, how learners with different performance levels engage in human-GenAI collaborative CPS remains underexplored. This study investigated the CPS processes of 25 students in Grades 4-6. Specifically, descriptive statistics and lag sequential analysis were used to examine students' behavioral characteristics and sequences, respectively, while epistemic network analysis was employed to investigate interaction qualities. The findings indicated that primary school students exhibited more behaviors in the idea generation and solution validation stages, reflecting a task-oriented behavioral pattern supported by GenAI. Significant differences in behavioral sequences across performance levels were observed: high-performing groups (HPGs) demonstrated clearer CPS goals and more continuous, progressive behavioral sequences, whereas low-performing groups (LPGs) showed less defined goals and more fragmented behavioral sequences. In terms of interaction qualities, HPGs were more effective at facilitating CPS through strategic questioning and the productive use of feedback, while LPGs were characterized by more superficial interactions. These findings provided theoretical insights and empirical evidence to inform human-GenAI collaborative CPS in primary education.

### 2. Generative AI use patterns and university students’ cognitive autonomy: roles of learning self-efficacy, disciplinary background, and institutional tier
- 저자: Xiuqin Zhang, Yong Gao, Wanyan Bao, Yunmiao Gui  |  게재: 2026-11-17
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2764522](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2764522)

**초록:**
As generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) becomes increasingly integrated into university learning, how students use it may matter more than how often they use it. Yet relationships between different GenAI use patterns and cognitive autonomy, and the psychological processes involved, remain insufficiently understood. This study examined how surface-oriented use and deep collaborative use relate to cognitive autonomy among university students, testing learning self-efficacy as an indirect pathway and disciplinary background and institutional tier as moderators. Data came from a questionnaire survey of university students (N = 419). Surface-oriented use was significantly and negatively associated with cognitive autonomy, whereas deep collaborative use was significantly and positively associated with it, most strongly for evaluative thinking. Learning self-efficacy showed significant indirect associations in both relationships. Disciplinary background and institutional tier also moderated the deep collaborative use-cognitive autonomy association, which was stronger among students in the social sciences, natural sciences, and engineering, and at Double First-Class universities. These findings suggest that how students engage with GenAI may be more relevant to cognitive autonomy than how often they use it. They provide evidence for context-sensitive AI-supported teaching in higher education.
