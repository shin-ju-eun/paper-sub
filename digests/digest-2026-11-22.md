## Interactive Learning Environments (1편)

### 1. The current perceptions of Saudi pre-service teachers towards the integration of artificial intelligence into early childhood education
- 저자: Jawaher Alghamdi, Fatma Mostafa  |  게재: 2026-11-21
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2764524](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2764524)

**초록:**
This study aims to examine the current perceptions of Saudi pre-service teachers regarding the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into early childhood education. The study employed an “explanatory sequential mixed-methods research design.” In the first (quantitative) phase, data were collected via a questionnaire from 114 pre-service teachers (students undergoing professional teacher preparation) in the Early Childhood Department at a university in the Eastern Province of Saudi Arabia. In the second (qualitative) phase, semi-structured interviews were conducted with a purposively selected subsample of these student teachers (n = 18). This qualitative phase was intended to analyze the quantitative findings concerning “AI-based personalized learning”. The results indicate that Saudi pre-service teachers have knowledge and a level of comfort and familiarity with the integration of technology and AI into general education. In terms of pre-service teachers’ perceptions towards the integration of AI in ECE settings, the results also indicate that they perceive AI in ECE as extremely useful. The potential benefits of integrating AI into ECE lie in classroom engagement and personalized learning. However, the results reported some factors that could affect AI integration in ECE, including a lack of experience, training, support, and clear guidance.
