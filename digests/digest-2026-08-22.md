## Interactive Learning Environments (2편)

### 1. AI-augmented supervision in shared chat spaces: how transparent, collaborative engagement with generative AI supports domain-specific critical engagement in project-based learning
- 저자: Rabih Kahaleh, Rodrigue Imad, Jihad Rishmany  |  게재: 2026-08-20
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2710356](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2710356)

**초록:**
The rapid integration of generative AI in higher education has often remained opaque, with students and instructors engaging AI privately and without shared accountability. This study examines AI-augmented supervision in shared chat spaces, designed to support domain-specific critical AI evaluation within project-based learning. An exploratory qualitative-dominant two-phase mixed-methods design was implemented in an undergraduate AI and business course at a private university in Lebanon (N = 26; third-year undergraduates aged 20–22; 15 females, 11 males). Phase 1 (weeks 3–7) used shared instructor–student–AI chat threads for individual supervision; Phase 2 (weeks 8–11) added rotating domain-related peer groups. Data included 26 chat transcripts (~600 coded dialogue moves), project revision traces, and a 25-item post-intervention survey. Transcripts were analyzed through interaction analysis and thematic coding (Cohen’s κ = 0.82–0.89). Findings reveal a supervision cycle of constraint setting, AI generation, critical evaluation, and student synthesis. Student-initiated evaluative moves rose from 18% to 38%, and peer participation was associated with substantive revisions in 11 of 26 projects (42%). Survey responses indicated high perceived transparency and educational value alongside bounded, context-specific trust. The study contributes evidence for triadic supervision design functioning as both supervision space and accountable decision log.

### 2. Cultivating preservice teachers’ GenAI competencies in a grammar methodology course
- 저자: Kemal Sinan Özmen, Eyüp Dilber, Esra Harmandaoğlu Baz  |  게재: 2026-08-20
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2715568](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2715568)

**초록:**
As preservice language teachers (PLTs) increasingly turn to generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) for planning, explanation, and materials design, teacher education programs face the challenge of making these uses pedagogically visible, ethically bounded, and professionally accountable. This study reports on the design and implementation of an eight-week module embedding GenAI into a core grammar methodology course. Grounded in Schön’s reflective practice and the Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge (TPACK) framework, the research investigates how PLTs appropriate GenAI as a drafting partner for lesson design, assessment, and ethical reflection. Participants included 32 PLTs in an English Language Teaching (ELT) program in Türkiye. Reflexive thematic analysis of weekly reflections, interviews, and course artifacts traced the development of PLTs’ professional judgment. Analysis revealed a developmental progression from instrumental experimentation to evidence-based, ethically bounded use. Participants advanced in prompt literacy, established routine verification, and adopted consistent disclosure habits. Crucially, they consolidated a teacher-first identity prioritizing pedagogical defensibility over technological novelty. The study offers a practice-proximal model for integrating GenAI into Second Language Teacher Education (SLTE). Embedding GenAI into core pedagogy, rather than isolated electives, can strengthen professional judgment, reflective accountability, and assessment literacy.
