## International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education (1편)

### 1. Where is the evidence? Mapping research on digitalization in higher education: A scoping review of German conference abstracts
- 저자: Anke Wischgoll, Christina Wekerle  |  게재: 2026-11-23
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00641-6](https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00641-6)

**초록:**
Digitalization has become a central topic in higher education, yet its empirical investigation remains fragmented. This study aims to map research patterns and potential gaps reflected in conference abstracts in the German higher education context using the C♭-model as a theoretical framework. A conference-based review was conducted, drawing on abstracts from major educational research conferences from 2023 to 2024. The selection and screening process followed an adapted PRISMA approach for scoping reviews, modified to account for conference-based data sources rather than database searches. Abstracts were analyzed using theory-driven and inductively developed coding categories, and descriptive methods as well as evidence gap maps were applied. The results reveal that digitalization is explicitly addressed in only a small proportion of abstracts. Research is strongly focused on student-related outcomes, particularly knowledge and skills, often combined with motivational variables. The main part of factors of the C♭-model are rarely examined. Most notably, learner engagement and learning activities, which constitute the conceptual core of the model through the ICAP framework, are largely absent from the analyzed abstracts. Overall, the findings indicate a fragmented research landscape with limited alignment between theoretical frameworks and empirical practice. By providing a systematic mapping of current research activities, this study contributes to a more nuanced understanding of digitalization in higher education and highlights critical areas requiring further empirical attention, especially on learner engagement.
