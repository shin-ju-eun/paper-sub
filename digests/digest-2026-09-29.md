## Interactive Learning Environments (1편)

### 1. From online interaction to creativity-related learning dispositions: a three-wave study of psychological need satisfaction
- 저자: Peng Wang, Yunan Sun  |  게재: 2026-09-27
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2734310](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2734310)

**초록:**
Online interaction offers students opportunities to exchange ideas and access learning resources, yet participation alone may not foster creativity-related learning dispositions. Less is known about the motivational experiences that connect learning-oriented digital participation with students' willingness to explore unfamiliar ideas and engage with challenging problems. Drawing on Self-Determination Theory, this study examined whether psychological need satisfaction helps explain the longitudinal association between digital participation and creativity-related learning dispositions. Three-wave survey data from undergraduates were analyzed using structural equation modeling. Learning-oriented digital participation was positively associated with subsequent psychological need satisfaction, encompassing autonomy, competence, and relatedness. Need satisfaction was, in turn, associated with stronger creativity-related learning dispositions, including intellectual risk-taking, curiosity, imagination, and orientation toward complexity and challenge. Direct longitudinal associations between specific forms of participation and these dispositions were inconsistent. The findings identify psychological need satisfaction as a more consistent explanatory pathway linking online interaction with creativity-related learning dispositions. They suggest that interactive learning environments may better support these dispositions when students experience meaningful choice, a sense of competence, and social connection. This motivational perspective helps explain why opportunities for online participation vary in their relevance to creativity-related learning.
