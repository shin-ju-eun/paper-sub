## Interactive Learning Environments (2편)

### 1. Conceptions of GenAI-assisted learning among university students with different levels of self-perceived AI literacy: a drawing-based content analysis and epistemic network analysis
- 저자: Xia Luo, Wei Zhou, Chunjie Zhang, Rui Zhang  |  게재: 2026-08-30
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2721390](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2721390)

**초록:**
As generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) becomes increasingly prevalent in higher education, understanding how students conceptualize its role in learning has become an important issue in AI education research. Existing studies have examined learning outcomes, usage intentions, and technology acceptance, but less is known about how students visually represent GenAI-supported learning and how these representations differ across levels of self-perceived AI literacy. This study combined drawing-based content analysis and epistemic network analysis (ENA) to examine drawings and self-report AI literacy data from 125 undergraduates at a university in Southwest China. Participants were divided into higher- and lower-scoring groups relative to the sample mean for exploratory network comparison. The higher-scoring group more often represented GenAI as a peer, depicted virtual-class settings, and included questioning, whereas the lower-scoring group more often represented GenAI as a tool and included information-search activities. ENA further showed different patterns of co-occurrence among the coded elements. These findings indicate an association between self-perceived AI literacy and selected visual representations of GenAI-supported learning, without implying differences in objectively demonstrated AI competence or actual learning behavior.

### 2. Driving mechanisms of students’ continuance intention to use AIDTs in co-creation contexts: a multi-method analysis using SEM–ANN–NCA
- 저자: Yi Rong, Zuowen Bao, Yangyan Huang, Yanqing Xia  |  게재: 2026-08-29
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2702022](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2702022)

**초록:**
With the continuous development of AIDTs, the relationship between students and AIDTs has shifted from one-way tool use to a more interactive and collaborative human–AI co-creation process. In co-creation contexts, the key factors influencing students’ continuance intention toward AIDTs, as well as the mechanisms through which these factors operate, remain insufficiently clarified. Based on this, the present study draws on the Motivation–Opportunity–Ability (MOA) framework and integrates Task–Technology Fit (TTF) and Information Systems Success (ISS) theories to develop a model of design students’ continuance intention toward AIDT-based co-creation. Using survey data collected from 371 Chinese university students, this study adopts a multi-method integration strategy and systematically validates the proposed model through CB-SEM, ANN, and NCA. The results indicate that, among the factors influencing students’ continuance intention in AIDT-based co-creation contexts, personal innovation and perceived utility exhibit particularly strong predictive effects. In addition, AI self-efficacy serves as a potential necessary condition for the development of a positive attitude, while attitude constitutes a necessary condition for students’ continuance intention. This study advances the understanding of human–AI co-creation in interactive learning environments and provides practical guidance for educators to foster more sustained, proactive, and high-quality human–AI co-creation.
