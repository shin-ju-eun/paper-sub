## Journal of Research on Technology in Education (1편)

### 1. Developing high school teachers’ self-efficacy for artificial intelligence through an AI literacy professional development program
- 저자: Valerie Riggs, Nathaniel Maukle  |  게재: 2026-08-13
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2704775](https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2704775)

**초록:**
Artificial intelligence is expanding in K–12 education, yet many teachers remain uncertain about how to use these tools responsibly in classroom practice. This study examined high school teachers’ AI self-efficacy following participation in an AI-focused professional development program. The analysis was guided by Bandura’s theory of self-efficacy and the Teachers’ AI Competency Scale (TAICS). Survey responses and open-ended reflections were collected from teachers participating in the second year of a summer AI research program at a historically Black college and university (HBCU). Teachers described confidence when using AI for planning and lesson design. Responses revealed less comfort related to assessment, ethics, human-centered use, and professional collaboration. Findings suggest that professional development must address multiple dimensions of AI competence to support responsible and equitable classroom use.
