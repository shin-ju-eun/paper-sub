## International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education (1편)

### 1. Mind the AI policy gap: a policy analysis across global funding bodies
- 저자: Cecilia Ka Yuk Chan  |  게재: 2026-11-04
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00637-2](https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00637-2)

**초록:**
Abstract Research funding bodies have significant influence on what research is prioritised, how it is conducted, and what standards are enforced. While a number of global frameworks provide normative guidance on AI ethics, there is a clear gap in understanding how these principles are implemented at the funding-body level. This study aims to examine the current AI policies and guidelines of research funding bodies across different geographical regions. Inductive thematic analysis was adopted to analyse 56 AI-related policies from 46 funding agencies across the US, the UK, Europe, Australia, Singapore, Mainland China, and Hong Kong. The analysis revealed five common themes: human-centredness, safeguarding, embeddedness, governance, and AI readiness. It was also found that current funding-specific policies lack explicit guidelines on how broad principles and macro-level regulations can be interpreted and applied in AI-enabled research funding processes. The findings underscore the important role of funding agencies as key actors in defining responsible AI use in research processes. Finally, the practical implications of this study for regulators, funding bodies, and researchers are discussed, highlighting the value of policy document analysis in shaping ethical, transparent, and responsible AI frameworks.

## Interactive Learning Environments (4편)

### 1. Applying the human-centric AI pedagogy framework: a collaborative self-study on a writing chatbot design in literacy education
- 저자: Shuling Yang, Carin Appleget  |  게재: 2026-11-03
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2754923](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2754923)

**초록:**
Guided by the Human-Centric Artificial Intelligence Pedagogy framework, two literacy teacher educators conducted a collaborative self-study of their experiences and co-created a writing chatbot on PlayLab that aligns with the Self-Regulated Strategy Development framework. Serving as critical friends, they documented and reflected on how their AI literacy grew iteratively throughout the design process. Findings show that, through individual reflection and collaborative dialogue, each educator deepened her understanding of how to align chatbot functionality with instructional purposes while keeping teachers’ and students’ writing agency at the center of the design. In this iterative, human-led process, AI prompted ongoing pedagogical inquiry rather than serving merely as a technological tool. The authors also surface ethical tensions between the chatbot’s capabilities and their instructional values. A pivotal episode, in which they redesigned the chatbot to stop generating full paragraphs, illustrates how pedagogical and ethical reasoning directly shaped the tool’s functionality. The authors suggest that pedagogical knowledge plays a critical role in human-centered AI design and that educators’ professional expertise is an essential foundation for designing AI tools for literacy education.
