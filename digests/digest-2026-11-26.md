## International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education (1편)

### 1. GenAI-supported self-explanation and its effects on metacognitive monitoring and critical reading comprehension: evidence from two experimental studies
- 저자: Caiyan Liu, Tao Xin, Zhihui Cai  |  게재: 2026-11-25
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00645-2](https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00645-2)

**초록:**
Generative AI (GenAI) holds considerable promise for supporting self-explanation and self-regulated learning (SRL), yet little is known about how different forms of GenAI-based support influence learning and metacognitive outcomes. Grounded in Zimmerman’s three-phase SRL model, the present research examined the effects of GenAI-based feedback and prompts embedded in self-explanation activities on critical reading comprehension, metacognitive monitoring, and cognitive load across two sequential experiments with Chinese university students. Study 1 (N = 119) compared three conditions in an integrative self-explanation context: self-explanation with personalized GenAI feedback (SE + FB), self-explanation only (SE), and a reading-only control. The SE + FB group demonstrated significantly better critical reading comprehension than both comparison groups and higher germane cognitive load than the control group. It also showed a negative monitoring bias (underestimation), whereas the comparison groups showed positive biases (overestimation), although absolute monitoring accuracy did not differ significantly across conditions. Study 2 (N = 113) compared scaffolded, standard, and no prompts provided before error-based self-explanations. Scaffolded prompts significantly improved self-explanation quality relative to both comparison groups and metacognitive monitoring accuracy relative to the no-prompt group. However, no significant differences in critical reading comprehension were observed across conditions. Together, these findings suggest that different forms of GenAI-based support for self-explanation may contribute to distinct cognitive and metacognitive outcomes, highlighting the importance of aligning support with specific learning activities to facilitate critical reading and self-regulated learning.

## Interactive Learning Environments (1편)

### 1. An online classroom teaching evaluation method integrating the wisdom of domain experts with the intelligent capabilities of large language models
- 저자: Jun Xiao, Mengting Liu, Feixiang Ge, Zhiran Li  |  게재: 2026-11-25
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2754911](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2754911)

**초록:**
Online classrooms have become an important teaching modality due to their real-time interactivity. However, existing teaching evaluation methods rely heavily on subjective observations and post-class questionnaires, resulting in limited efficiency, objectivity, and timeliness. Large language models (LLMs) offer new opportunities for multimodal teaching evaluation, but their susceptibility to hallucinations may undermine evaluation reliability and decision validity. Integrating expert knowledge with LLM capabilities offers a promising solution, yet effective human-AI collaboration remains underexplored. To address this gap, this study proposes an online classroom teaching evaluation framework comprising a multimodal evaluation indicator system and a human-AI collaborative assessment method integrating LLMs with expert intelligence. The framework was validated through case studies, questionnaires, and interviews. Results demonstrate that the proposed framework generates reliable and interpretable teaching evaluation reports. Teachers and learners perceive these reports as valuable for analyzing teaching behaviors, optimizing instructional content, and improving learning experiences. This study contributes to the development of human-AI collaborative teaching evaluation and provides practical guidance for achieving more objective, efficient, and evidence-based online classroom assessment.
