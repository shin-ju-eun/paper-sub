## Interactive Learning Environments (1편)

### 1. Beyond technology acceptance: students’ perceptions and prompt engineering competence in higher education
- 저자: María Consuelo Sáiz Manzanares, María del Camino Escolar Llamazares, Irene González-Díez, Gloria Perez-Lopez-de-Echazarreta, Carmen Varela et al.  |  게재: 2026-10-29
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2744402](https://doi.org/10.1080/10494820.2026.2744402)

**초록:**
The integration of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) into Higher Education requires understanding students' perceptions and competence in interacting with these technologies. This mixed-methods study examined perceptions of GenAI and documented prompt engineering competence in academic tasks among 89 Psychology and Biomedical Engineering students. Study 1 employed a 2 × 2 quasi-experimental factorial design comparing disciplinary area and instructional approach (guided vs. autonomous), complemented by thematic analysis. Students reported favourable views of GenAI, particularly for understanding, learning, guidance, and work planning, while emphasising the need to verify AI-generated information and improve reliability and contextual accuracy. No significant multivariate effects of disciplinary area, instructional approach, or their interaction were found. However, item-level analyses identified two items with higher scores under autonomous instruction after multiple-comparison adjustment. Study 2 examined documented prompt engineering competence in 24 group reports using five rubric dimensions and the exploratory Prompt Competence Index (PCI). No differences between instructional approaches remained significant after multiple-comparison adjustment, although descriptive differences in Iteration and PCI emerged among biomedical engineering reports. Overall, findings support contextualised, iterative, and critically supervised human–AI interaction, while suggesting that instructional differences are context- and indicator-specific. Further validation of the PCI in larger, diverse, and balanced samples is required.

## Journal of Computer Assisted Learning (1편)

### 1. Assessing the Impact of Immersive Technologies on Academic Performance, Cognitive Load, Engagement, and Self‐Regulated Learning: A Quasi‐Experimental Study in Kazakhstan
- 저자: Olga Tapalova, Nadezhda Zhiyenbayeva, Zholdybay Uspanov  |  게재: 2026-10-29
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1002/jcal.70358](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcal.70358)

**초록:**
ABSTRACT Background The relevance of this study stems from the digital transformation of Kazakhstan's higher education system and the need for pedagogically sound immersive technologies: virtual ( VR ) and augmented realities ( AR ), capable of improving the quality of education under conditions of limited educational resources. Objectives This quasi‐experimental study aims to assess the impact of VR/AR technologies on the academic achievement and psychological characteristics (i.e., cognitive load, engagement, and self‐regulated learning strategies) of students pursuing a degree in pedagogy, psychology, engineering, and technology at universities in Kazakhstan. Methods This study used a quasi‐experimental design with control and experimental groups and conducted pre‐ and pos t ‐tests to assess the effect of the intervention. The study also used a cognitive load scale and qualitative interviews. Results and Conclusions Results showed a significant increase in academic performance in the VR/AR group (Δ = +22.7%–24.4%; Cohen's d = 1.54–1.78; p < 0.001) as compared to the control group ( d ≈0.8–0.9). The overall cognitive load did not increase, but scores for performance (81.5 vs. 73.8; p < 0.001), effort (69.2 vs. 60.7; p < 0.001), and frustration (25.3 vs. 28.9; p = 0.032) were higher. Statistically significant differences were found within the VR/AR group in terms of engagement and self‐regulated learning strategies ( p < 0.001). A positive correlation was found between engagement and self‐regulation ( r = 0.62), whereas a negative correlation was present between engagement and cognitive load ( r = −0.28…−0.31). VR/AR usage had a significant moderating effect (η 2 ≈0.39–0.42), whereas the moderation effect of university affiliation was insignificant. The proposed conceptual framework has demonstrated its empirical applicability in this study. The results obtained, taking contextual limitations into account, can serve as an empirical basis for the future implementation of virtual and augmented reality solutions.

## Journal of Research on Technology in Education (4편)

### 1. Bridging computer science and data science in preservice teacher education through integration design
- 저자: Lin Chu, Anne Todd Ottenbreit-Leftwich  |  게재: 2026-10-29
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2737299](https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2737299)

**초록:**
This study investigated preservice teachers’ perception and attitudes toward data science education, AI literacy, and teacher data use following a three-week instructional intervention grounded in computer science and integration design. Using a mixed-methods approach, the study analyzed questionnaire data, artifacts, and interviews. Results showed a significant improvement in preservice teachers’ attitudes and literacy with some differences between the PreK-elementary and secondary groups. The findings provided practical justification for a CS-grounded integration design for data science education in preservice teacher training.

### 2. Beyond school support: Internalizing the school innovation support system in teachers’ digital teaching innovation
- 저자: Qinghao Li  |  게재: 2026-10-29
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2737297](https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2737297)

**초록:**
Promoting teachers’ digital teaching innovation has become a key challenge in AI-enhanced junior secondary education, particularly under high-stakes examination pressures. This study uses structural equation modeling, Shapley value decomposition, and fuzzy-set qualitative comparative analysis to examine how the school innovation support system shapes teachers’ digital teaching innovation. The findings show that the school innovation support system does not directly translate into digital teaching innovation. Instead, its influence is mainly transmitted through teachers’ AI readiness and teaching self-efficacy, with AI readiness serving as the strongest pathway. Shapley value decomposition shows that the school innovation support system is not a purely top-down mechanism. Vertical leadership support plays a slightly stronger enabling role, while horizontal peer support remains almost equally consequential in sustaining teachers’ digital teaching innovation. The configurational results further show that high digital teaching innovation can emerge through multiple pathways, while AI readiness and teaching self-efficacy consistently serve as core conditions.

### 3. “I trained every part of myself beneath these technological wrappings”: exploring and defining foreign language PK–12 teachers’ critical multilingual and multimodal AI competence
- 저자: Huixin Wang, Yunan Zhang, Jiyou Jia  |  게재: 2026-10-29
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2740580](https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2740580)

**초록:**
Teachers’ AI competence is increasingly essential for language teaching with the rise of GenAI. Existing teacher AI competence frameworks, however, do not fully capture their temporal and dynamic nature, nor how teachers engage with GenAI through multilingual, multimodal, and social-contextually situated practices. This longitudinal qualitative study examines how multilingual teachers assess, perceive and develop AI competence, and how socio-technical, multilingual contexts shape it over time. Data were collected through journey plots, multimodal artifacts and semi-structured interviews. Findings reveal teachers’ multidimensional AI competence shaped by linguistic repertoires, cultural positioning, and ethical reflection. A domain-specific linguistically and culturally responsive teacher AI competence framework, called critical multilingual and multimodal AI competence, is proposed.

### 4. A mixed-methods study on generative AI’s influence on problem-solving mechanisms among pre-service teachers
- 저자: Yun-Qi Bai  |  게재: 2026-10-29
- 링크: [https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2736124](https://doi.org/10.1080/15391523.2026.2736124)

**초록:**
This mixed-methods study examines how a generative AI (GAI)-supported intervention reshapes pre-service teachers’ (PSTs) problem-solving. Using an explanatory sequential design, 108 PSTs were assigned to no-technology, search engine, or GAI-supported conditions. Quantitatively, the GAI-supported group demonstrated significantly higher metacognition and self-efficacy and lower cognitive load. Qualitatively, thematic analysis revealed a tripartite mechanism: externalizing metacognitive reflection, offloading cognitive tasks, and fostering confidence partially contingent on the AI partner—termed Externalized Self-Efficacy. These findings suggest that while GAI-supported collaboration offers cognitive-affective benefits, it also risks efficacy-source externalization, underscoring the need for teacher education to cultivate critical human-AI partnerships that safeguard endogenous professional agency.
